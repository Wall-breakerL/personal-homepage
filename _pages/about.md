I am **Huajun Lu (陆华均)**, a Computer Science undergraduate at **Nanjing University**. My interests include AI for Science, computational ethology, and language-model reasoning.

I enjoy turning research questions into working systems, with particular attention to data quality, reproducible experiments, and tools that make results easier to inspect.

## Research and projects

My work has included:

- **AutoEtho:** pose-data quality checks and tools for reviewing cluster annotations in mouse-behavior analysis.
- **PPL-guided reasoning-tree exploration:** an empirical study of perplexity and Monte Carlo value estimates in language-model reasoning.
- **Human-AI Performance Lab:** a web application for exploring human–AI collaboration styles.

## Background

Algorithm competitions have been an important part of my training. As a member of the team *平流层*, I received gold medals at the ICPC Xi'an and Kunming regional contests and the CCPC Qinhuangdao regional contest.

I have also been involved in community programming education and support for older adults using digital devices through Nanjing University's Computer Science Youth Volunteer Association.
