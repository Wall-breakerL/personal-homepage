## AutoEtho

**Computational ethology · Data quality · Human review**

AutoEtho investigates a workflow for mouse-behavior analysis, connecting pose estimation, feature extraction, clustering, language-model annotation, and human review.

My contributions have focused on identifying and cleaning unreliable pose estimates, visually checking the data, and developing a cluster-label review tool that links semantic scatter plots with representative video clips.

A related public repository provides a standalone single-mouse pose-processing pipeline:

[superanimal-pipeline →](https://github.com/Wall-breakerL/superanimal-pipeline)

## PPL-guided reasoning-tree exploration

**Language-model reasoning · Reinforcement learning · Empirical analysis**

This exploratory work asks whether perplexity can serve as a lightweight signal for deciding where to branch a reasoning tree. I implemented an analysis of the relationship between answer-based perplexity and Monte Carlo value estimates, and prepared the experimental report.

The observed correlation was preliminary evidence for further investigation, rather than a demonstrated improvement in end-to-end reasoning performance.

[Analysis code →](https://github.com/Wall-breakerL/treerl-ppl-v-corr)
